How to Prevent Emergency Water Damage Restoration Problems

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Know where your electrical panel is so power to wet rooms can be cut safely.
  • Store valuables and important papers off the floor, especially in basements and lower levels.
  • Replace old supply hoses on washers, toilets and faucets before they burst.
  • Locate the main shutoff valve now, and show each person at home how to turn it off.
  • Sinks, dishwashers, washing machines and water heaters are all good spots for a leak sensor.
  • Test your sump pump before the rainy season and consider a battery backup.

Signs You Need Emergency Water Damage Restoration in Seaside

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • The breaker box, a running appliance or cords along the floor sit in the path of the water.
  • A leak upstairs is sending water down the walls of the level beneath it.
  • Standing water is spreading across floors toward other rooms or down the stairs.
  • The house was flooded when you walked in, with no telling how many hours it had been going.
  • Part of your ceiling bows downward because water is pooling on top of it.
  • Sewage has backed up, or the water is dark with a foul odor.
  • You see water escaping around a light fitting, from a wall outlet or through the ceiling.
